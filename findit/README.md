# Знайди річ (findit)

Hidden-object mini-game for *One Day in Kyiv*. The room is a mess; find 6 things: a blanket, a power bank, fairy lights, batteries, a pillow, and the power bank's charger.

- The items are partly covered by clutter.
- Tap to find an item; a miss counts as a miss.
- Every 14–24 s the power goes out for 7–11 s and the screen goes dark except for a flashlight circle under your finger or mouse. In the dark you aim the light and release to grab.
- The timer counts up; the win screen shows your time and misses, then Play again.
- UA/EN switch, pause button, ☰ Меню link.
- Low-res 120×170 canvas upscaled with pixelated rendering. Everything is static: no stripes or scrolling patterns.
- Test API: `window.__findit` (`state`, `tap`, `blackout`, `setPtr`).
