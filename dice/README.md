# Кості світла (dice)

Warm-up mini-game for *One Day in Kyiv*. Guess 1–24: how many hours of electricity today. Roll a white die (hours with power) and a black die (hours without), paint the 24-hour outage-schedule grid from 00:00 until midnight (last segment cut at 24:00), then compare light hours with the guess. UA/EN, calm animations (no stripes).

Live: https://mikerudine.github.io/kyiv-slots/dice/
